# AI-Assisted Development Record

## Purpose
Track AI use so the repository has a clear provenance trail for future commercial due diligence.

## Tools reported by the owner
- Perplexity AI: used to produce development code; generated material has been or will be modified and integrated by the creator.
- ChatGPT: used for research, drafting, engineering review, troubleshooting and development assistance.

## Human authorship boundary
The creator remains responsible for selecting, modifying, integrating, testing and maintaining the repository. AI assistance is not treated as a substitute for human review.

## Commercial review rule
Before selling or licensing the template, review AI-assisted code and content for:
1. third-party code similarity or copied snippets;
2. licence obligations attached to any referenced source;
3. provider terms applicable to generated output;
4. accidental inclusion of proprietary/client material;
5. generated claims, assets or content that should be replaced.

## Current status
AI provenance is documented at the tool level, but individual lines of code are not currently tagged by source. This is acceptable as an operational record for now, but a future commercial release should include a final provenance review.
