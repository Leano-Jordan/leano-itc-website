03 / Operating loop

Understand. Design. Build. Verify.

The same engineering loop applies to a new feature, a bug sweep, a workflow improvement or a full software review.

01

Understand

Map the workflow, inspect the implementation and identify the real constraint before changing it.

02

Design

Choose the smallest architecture and interface change that actually solves the problem.

03

Build

Implement focused batches while preserving existing behaviour and shared-code consumers.

04

Verify

Check syntax, behaviour, responsiveness and quality before calling the work finished.
